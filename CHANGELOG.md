## [0.1.0] - 2026-06-22

### Added
- Curated directory of 26 Document AI & OCR tools across 4 categories (OCR
  engines, document parsers, layout/table extraction, managed APIs), with
  open-source and license metadata.
- `doc-dir` CLI: list (filters: category/open-source), search, categories,
  stats, export (json/csv), generate.
- Auto-generated README, registry-integrity tests, and CI (incl. README-in-sync check).
